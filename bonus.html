Superhero Team: Yellow Eagle and Yarrow

Yellow Eagle can fly fast and shoot yellow laser beams. His sidekick, Yarrow, uses plant magic to heal friends and catch bad guys.

Arch-Nemeses

  • Dr. Chrono
    • Major Superpower: Rewinds time by 2 minutes.
    • Minor Superpower: Makes butterflies appear.
  • Lady Rust
    • Major Superpower: Bends metal with her mind.
    • Minor Superpower: Changes her eye color when blinking.
